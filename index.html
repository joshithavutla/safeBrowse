Safe Browsing & Smart Search Guide

Learn how to browse the internet safely, search smarter and protect your personal information.

Start Learning

🛡️ Safe Browsing

Safe browsing means using the internet carefully while protecting your device, accounts and personal information.

🔍

Check the Website

Check the website address carefully before entering passwords or personal information.

🔒

Look for HTTPS

Check that websites handling sensitive information use HTTPS.

⚠️

Avoid Suspicious Links

Be careful with unexpected links in emails, messages and social media.

🔄

Keep Software Updated

Keep your browser and operating system updated to improve security.

🔎 Smart Search

Smart searching means using specific keywords and reliable sources to find useful information quickly.

❌ Less Effective Search

chemistry pollution

This search is very broad and may produce many unrelated results.

✅ More Effective Search

effects of water pollution on aquatic ecosystems

This search is more specific and helps find focused information.

Smart Search Techniques

🎯

Use Specific Keywords

Use words that clearly describe what information you are looking for.

🔤

Use Exact Phrases

Put an exact phrase inside quotation marks when you want results containing that phrase.

📚

Check Reliable Sources

Prefer trustworthy educational, scientific and official sources.

📅

Check the Date

Check when information was published or updated, especially for changing topics.

🔐 Privacy & Personal Data

Protecting personal information is an important part of staying safe online.

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for your important accounts.

👤

Limit Personal Information

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information such as your address or private details online.

⚙️

Check Privacy Settings

Review privacy settings on websites, apps and social media accounts.

📱

Be Careful on Public Devices

Always sign out of accounts when using a shared or public computer.
